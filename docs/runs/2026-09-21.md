## What stands, and what does not

- **Measured:** the halo is alive only on the tick of a ping and destroyed on every sample taken later; off screen the job
  pings once every 15 ticks, on screen every tick. The game's own IL (`Mote.TimeInterval`) explains it: a `needsMaintenance`
  mote is destroyed on any tick after its last maintained one, `lastMaintainTick` starts at 0 and only `Maintain()` writes it.
- **Not shown:** that the fix works, that the halo is drawn (the software renderer may not draw the distortion shader),
  that a sound is heard.
