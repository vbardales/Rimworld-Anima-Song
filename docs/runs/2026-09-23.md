## What stands, and what does not

- **Measured, and now fixed:** the halo was alive only on the tick of a ping. Two causes, in the game's own IL
  (`Mote.TimeInterval`): a `needsMaintenance` mote is destroyed on any tick after its last maintained one, with no slack,
  and `lastMaintainTick` starts at 0 (fresh mote never maintained: `b8d7f25`); and the listener's `tickIntervalAction`
  does not ping on every tick at higher speeds (`HaloKeeper`, `c04edcc`).
- **The halo holds at 1x, 3x and 15x on the tree in view** (three passes, `c04edcc`), in English, French and with Phytokin.
- **The whole suite has now run on the final mod build in the three passes**: French and Phytokin fully green, English green
  but for one test fault since corrected and rerun.
- **Not shown:** that the halo is drawn (the software renderer may not draw the distortion shader), that a sound is heard,
  that Phytokin's ability soothes when cast, `baseChance`, removing the mod from a save.
